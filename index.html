Lab bench to market

I'm a bioengineer who can't leave a good idea in the lab. Microbial fuel cells. A bacteria detector for food safety. A bio-electrical fix for sour gas. Each one started as research and turned into a company.

Then I learned the other side of the table: licensing Harvard research, an HBS MBA, early-stage investing at Applied Ventures. After that I built in hardware, blockchain and ecommerce, and ran a respirator materials maker through its sale in 2020.

Now I do it inside a manufacturer, working on innovation at IPG.

Fourteen roles, two acquisitions, one habit: find the science worth scaling, then build the business around it.
