Science, then market

I started at a lab bench, building microbial fuel cells at a Harvard spinout. I've spent the years since moving ideas from research into businesses that ship.

That path has run through two biotech startups, licensing Harvard research, early-stage venture investing, and founding hardware, blockchain and ecommerce companies. I also led a meltblown materials manufacturer through its acquisition.

Today I work on innovation at IPG, the North American tape and packaging manufacturer.
